DATA DICTIONARY - BaseBEDescriptive.xlsx
================================

VARIABLE DESCRIPTIONS:
----------------------

1. age
   Description: Age group of the respondent
   Coding:
     - 1 = 17-27 
     - 2 = 28-40 
     - 3 = >40 

2. gender
   Description: Gender of the respondent
   Coding:
     - 1 = Male
     - 2 = Female

3. academic_level
   Description: Highest educational level attained
   Coding:
     - 1 = Primary
     - 2 = Secondary
     - 3 = High School
     - 4 = Bachelor
     - 5 = Master or PhD degree

NOTES:
------
- All variables are categorical (nominal or ordinal)
- The dataset contains responses from multiple individuals
- Age groups are ordinal (increasing with age)
- Academic level is ordinal (increasing with educational attainment)
- Gender is nominal (no inherent order)
